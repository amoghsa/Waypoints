# Waypoints

**Plan the trip you want. Let the charging disappear into it.**

Waypoints is a native iOS and iPadOS EV road-trip planner that fits DC fast-charging into the stops you already want to make — meals, playground breaks, hotels, arrival times — instead of treating charging as the thing your whole trip revolves around. It also learns your real dwell habits over time, so each plan leans on how long you actually stay rather than just what you scheduled. Everything is stored and computed entirely on your device: there is no backend, no account, and no sync.

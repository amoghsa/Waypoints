# Waypoints — Data Deletion

**Effective date:** 2026-07-16
**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

This page describes every way to delete data associated with your use of Waypoints (iOS and iPadOS). It is published in addition to the full [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) so that the deletion options are easy to find, and to satisfy Google Play's user-data-deletion requirement (see https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111).

If you have any trouble deleting data, email **waypoints@amoghsa.com** or open an issue at https://github.com/amoghsa/Waypoints/issues — we'll respond within 30 days.

## Does Waypoints have accounts?

**No.** Waypoints has no sign-up, no login, no username, no password, and no profile that lives on a server we operate. There is no backend and no cloud sync. Because nothing you enter ever leaves your device, there is no account or server-side record to delete — only the data stored locally on your device, which you can remove yourself at any time.

## Where your data is stored

Everything Waypoints holds is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, inside the app's own sandbox, plus a small set of preferences in the standard iOS preferences store. This includes your trips and their stops, your household profile, the vehicle you've selected, and the dwell habits the app learns as you travel. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

## How to delete your data

You have three options, from most targeted to most complete:

- **Delete a single trip — Trips list → swipe to delete.** Swiping a trip away removes that trip and all of its stops. Nothing else is affected.
- **Forget learned habits only — Settings → Forget my habits.** This deletes every dwell observation the app has recorded (the planned-versus-actual timing behind its suggestions) while keeping your trips, household profile, and vehicle choice intact.
- **Erase everything in the app — Settings → Reset all data.** This deletes all of your on-device data — trips, stops, learned habits, household profile, vehicle selection, and the app's built-in charger and point-of-interest catalog — clears the app's stored preferences, and returns Waypoints to its first-run state.

## Full removal

To remove Waypoints and everything it has stored from your device entirely, **uninstall the app**. Deleting the app removes its local database and all of its preferences. Since there is no cloud copy and no account, uninstalling leaves nothing behind on any server we operate.

## Data held by booking partners

Waypoints has no account and no server-side record of you. However, if you followed a **"Find a place to stay"** link (Stay22) or an **"Open in … app"** charging-network link and then booked or signed in on that partner's site, any account or booking you created lives with **that** company, not with Waypoints — we cannot see or delete it. To remove data held by a partner, use that company's own privacy/deletion process (for Stay22, see https://www.stay22.com/privacy-policy). Deleting or uninstalling Waypoints does not affect anything stored by a partner.
